How the optimizer works

What it does

Your PDF is rewritten by Ghostscript running inside your browser: oversized images are downsampled and recompressed, fonts are subset, and content streams are rewritten. Text and vector graphics are never rasterized — the output stays selectable and searchable.

Privacy

Unlike online "compress PDF" services, nothing is uploaded. The compression engine (~14 MB) is downloaded once from a CDN the first time you optimize a file; your PDF itself never leaves your machine.

Quality presets

Smallest downsamples images to 72 dpi — best for documents you'll only read on screen. Balanced (150 dpi) is the recommended default. High quality (300 dpi) touches images gently and prints well.

Honest results

Some PDFs — especially book scans already compressed with JBIG2 — are near-optimal and can even grow when reprocessed. The optimizer always shows original vs. result size, and if the result isn't smaller it says so instead of handing you a worse file.

Limits

Password-protected PDFs can't be processed. Very large files (hundreds of MB) may exceed the browser's memory — if that happens you'll get a clear error, not a broken download.
